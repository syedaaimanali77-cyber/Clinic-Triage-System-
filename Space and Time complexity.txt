1. Why does LinkedQueue keep a tail reference? What would Enqueue cost without it?

In linkedQueue tail is the last Node of the queue. For instance in our linked queue file
we use 
       --------------------
        tail.Next=newNode; 
          tail=newNode; 
       --------------------  
       
coz we already know the last node of queue so, we can add new patient directly .
with tail complexity is O(1) bcz the work stays same even if the number of patients increases.
without tail the time complexity is O(n) bcz when the patients increases the work increases as well 
we use this when we have to search a patient registry one by one.
